# Supplier Audit Frequency Rules

## Purpose

This document defines audit recency expectations for suppliers included in the sustainability risk review process.

## Standard Audit Frequency

Supplier sustainability audits should be refreshed at least every 12 months.

A recent audit helps confirm that supplier sustainability performance, documentation, and corrective actions are still current.

## Outdated Audit

A supplier should be flagged for review if the most recent audit is older than 12 months.

An outdated audit may increase the supplier's risk classification because the available information may no longer reflect the supplier's current sustainability performance.

## Missing Audit Date

If the supplier audit date is missing, the agent should flag the record as incomplete and recommend requesting updated audit information.

## Follow-Up Actions

If a supplier audit is outdated or missing, recommended actions include:

* Request an updated sustainability audit.
* Schedule a supplier review meeting.
* Ask the supplier to provide current ESG performance documentation.
* Add the supplier to a quarterly monitoring list until the audit is updated.

## Decision-Support Guidance

Audit recency should be evaluated together with ESG scores, certification status, and supplier notes. An outdated audit does not automatically disqualify a supplier, but it should trigger additional review before making strategic sourcing decisions.
