# Supplier ESG Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the sustainability expectations for suppliers included in the supplier risk review process. The goal is to support responsible sourcing decisions by evaluating environmental, labor, waste, water, certification, and audit-related risk factors.

## Supplier Sustainability Expectations

Suppliers should maintain acceptable performance across the following ESG categories:

* Emissions management
* Labor practices
* Waste management
* Water management
* Sustainability or environmental certification
* Timely audit documentation

Suppliers with missing documentation, outdated audits, or repeated low sustainability scores should be reviewed before being approved for strategic sourcing or preferred supplier status.

## Risk Review Triggers

A supplier should be flagged for additional review if one or more of the following conditions are present:

* Any sustainability score is below the minimum acceptable threshold.
* Certification status is missing.
* Certification status is pending and other risk indicators are present.
* The most recent supplier audit is older than 12 months.
* Supplier notes indicate unresolved sustainability gaps.
* Multiple ESG categories show below-target performance.

## Decision-Support Guidance

Supplier ESG risk analysis should be used as decision support. A high-risk classification does not automatically disqualify a supplier. Instead, it indicates that the supplier may require additional documentation, an updated audit, a corrective action plan, or closer monitoring.

Final procurement, compliance, legal, and sustainability decisions should remain with qualified human reviewers.
