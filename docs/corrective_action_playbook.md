# Supplier Corrective Action Playbook

## Purpose

This playbook defines recommended follow-up actions for suppliers identified as Medium Risk or High Risk during sustainability review.

## General Follow-Up Actions

When a supplier has sustainability gaps, the reviewer should choose follow-up actions based on the specific risk drivers identified.

Common follow-up actions include:

* Request updated sustainability documentation.
* Request valid environmental certification.
* Schedule a supplier sustainability audit.
* Require a corrective action plan.
* Add the supplier to quarterly monitoring.
* Ask the supplier to provide evidence of improvement.

## Emissions-Related Actions

If the supplier has a low emissions score, recommended actions include:

* Request an emissions reduction plan.
* Ask for recent emissions reporting.
* Require improvement targets and timelines.
* Monitor progress in future supplier reviews.

## Labor-Related Actions

If the supplier has a low labor score, recommended actions include:

* Request labor practice documentation.
* Ask for evidence of worker safety and compliance processes.
* Schedule a labor practices review.
* Require a corrective action plan for unresolved labor concerns.

## Waste-Related Actions

If the supplier has a low waste score, recommended actions include:

* Request a waste reduction plan.
* Ask for recycling, reuse, or disposal process documentation.
* Require progress updates on waste improvement initiatives.
* Monitor waste performance in future reviews.

## Water-Related Actions

If the supplier has a low water score, recommended actions include:

* Request a water usage reduction plan.
* Ask for water management documentation.
* Require improvement targets for high water usage.
* Monitor water performance in future supplier reviews.

## Certification-Related Actions

If certification is missing or pending, recommended actions include:

* Request updated certification documentation.
* Confirm whether certification is expired, unavailable, or still in progress.
* Require supporting evidence before approving preferred supplier status.
* Track the supplier until certification status is resolved.

## Audit-Related Actions

If the latest audit is outdated or missing, recommended actions include:

* Request an updated supplier sustainability audit.
* Schedule a supplier review.
* Ask for current ESG documentation.
* Add the supplier to quarterly monitoring.

## Executive Summary Guidance

The agent should summarize corrective actions in clear business language. Recommendations should be prioritized based on the strongest risk drivers and should avoid unsupported claims.
