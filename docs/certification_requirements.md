# Supplier Certification Requirements

## Purpose

This document defines certification expectations for suppliers included in the sustainability risk review process.

## Preferred Certification Status

Preferred suppliers should maintain valid sustainability or environmental certification, such as ISO 14001 or an equivalent recognized certification.

Certification helps indicate that a supplier has formal environmental management practices, documented procedures, and a repeatable process for monitoring sustainability performance.

## Missing Certification

If a supplier's certification status is missing, the supplier should be flagged for follow-up. Missing certification should trigger a documentation request and may increase the supplier's overall risk classification.

Recommended follow-up actions include:

* Request updated sustainability or environmental certification.
* Ask the supplier to confirm whether certification is unavailable, expired, or not yet submitted.
* Require supporting documentation before approving preferred supplier status.
* Add the supplier to a monitoring list until documentation is complete.

## Pending Certification

If certification is pending, the supplier may be classified as Medium Risk unless other high-risk indicators are present. Pending certification should be tracked until a final status is available.

## Decision-Support Guidance

Certification status should be evaluated alongside ESG scores, audit recency, and supplier notes. Certification alone should not be the only factor used to determine supplier sustainability risk.
